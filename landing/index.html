MAC 3D · How to Capture

Four steps. One clean print.

Lay your thumb flat on a dark surface and hold the phone about 15–20cm above it. The phone orbits your thumb — your thumb stays still. When the circle turns green, hold steady and it captures itself. Four steps, around 14 seconds, no buttons to press.

What makes it lock
center_focus_strong Focus

Fill the frame with your thumb so the camera locks onto the ridges — not the background. Sharp ridges, no blur.

wb_sunny Lighting

Bright, even light works best. Avoid harsh glare and deep shadow — both wash out the ridge detail.

pan_tool Steadiness

Settle on each angle and hold still for a moment. Don't rush the move — jitter resets the lock, so let it settle.
